Voxtral 3 opens separate transcription and speech-generation previews

Mistral's model documentation lists Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 and Voxtral TTS 3 Mini as public API previews. They serve different speech workflows and use different billing units: audio minutes for transcription and characters for speech generation. Preview documentation and availability should be checked for the exact variant.

Sources: Mistral AI·Mistral AI·Mistral AI
Models15 Oct 2026· 3 source links

Timestral offers four forecasting checkpoints for local inference

Mistral released four Timestral checkpoints for time-series forecasting, with downloadable weights and local inference code. The family spans 5M to 209M parameters. Its reported context is measured in time steps, rather than language-model tokens, and its benchmark table is a vendor result rather than an independent comparison.

ASML, Amadeus and others commit support for Mistral's European data center expansion

Mistral said companies including ASML and Amadeus have committed to support its planned European data center buildout, adding named enterprise backing to the infrastructure strategy it recently outlined. The reported commitments strengthen Mistral's push to secure long-term customers and partners as it targets large-scale sovereign AI compute capacity in Europe.
