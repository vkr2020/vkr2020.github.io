Ailytics raises $7.4M Series A to bring vision AI to existing industrial cameras

Ailytics raised $7.4 million in a Series A led by Tin Men Capital, with SEEDS and Safie among the participants. The Singapore company applies vision AI to existing industrial CCTV and plans expansion across Asia-Pacific, the US and Gulf markets. Its deployment counts and claimed safety improvements are company-reported rather than independently measured results.
