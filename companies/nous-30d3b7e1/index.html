Nous raises $17M to expand its household AI agent beyond the UK

Nous announced $17 million in new funding from Heartcore Capital, Mosaic Ventures and other investors. Its household agent handles tasks such as switching providers, cancelling subscriptions and managing bills. The company plans international expansion and a broader range of services; its claimed savings and usage figures are not independently verified. The release does not name a round stage or financing instrument.
