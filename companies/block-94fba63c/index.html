Block previews Dessa, a voice model it says was trained directly on human conversations

Block has introduced Dessa, a speech-to-speech model it says was trained from human conversations without a text model. The company reports local operation on an M4 Max MacBook Pro or newer and is inviting a limited group of safety researchers to test it. This is a research preview, not confirmed general availability.

Sources: Block·The Decoder

Block open-sources Berd, a desktop workspace for AI agents across models and tools

Block has open-sourced Berd, a desktop application it originally built for internal use as a unified workspace for AI agents, models, files, and projects. The app is available on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license with downloadable builds for macOS, Windows, and Linux, and Block says it stores conversation history locally while working directly with local files, repositories, and tools.
