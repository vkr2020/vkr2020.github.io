Alphabet raises $85 billion to expand Google’s AI infrastructure

First recorded June 1, 2026 · Latest coverage June 3, 2026 · Developing over 3 days · 1 source

Materially updated June 3, 2026

TechCrunch reports that Alphabet completed an $85 billion stock sale tied to funding Google’s AI business and infrastructure expansion. The financing is framed around surging demand for Google’s AI products and services, with the new report describing the raise as larger and apparently completed rather than previously planned.

Why it matters: An $85 billion capital raise by Alphabet is a major signal that hyperscalers expect AI demand and infrastructure spending to remain exceptionally high. It reinforces that datacenter capacity, chips, and power-backed buildouts are now strategic constraints in AI competition, and that public markets remain willing to fund them at extraordinary scale.
