ShieldFont launches a font designed to poison AI web scraping while remaining readable to humans

First recorded August 12, 2026 · Latest coverage August 12, 2026 · 1 source

Ars Technica reports on ShieldFont, a newly introduced font intended to make scraped webpage text unreliable for AI systems while keeping pages legible to human readers. The tool is presented as an anti-scraping defense aimed at disrupting training-data collection from the open web rather than blocking access outright.

Why it matters: ShieldFont reflects a new class of technical countermeasures in the conflict over AI data collection, where site owners seek to degrade the usefulness of scraped content instead of relying only on robots.txt, contracts, or litigation. If adopted widely, such tools could raise costs for model developers, worsen data quality, and intensify the broader fight over consent and control in training data.
