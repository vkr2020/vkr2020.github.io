xpander.ai makes its enterprise AI agent control-plane platform generally available

First recorded August 17, 2026 · Latest coverage August 17, 2026 · 1 source

xpander.ai said its enterprise platform for building, running, and governing AI agents is now generally available. The company positions the product as a vendor-neutral control and context layer that sits above models, agent frameworks, and infrastructure, adding execution controls, permissions, observability, memory, enterprise system access, and lifecycle management.

Why it matters: The launch is a signal that agent governance and orchestration are becoming a standalone enterprise software layer rather than features tied to a single model or cloud provider. If customers adopt neutral control planes, they could gain more flexibility in managing large fleets of agents across mixed AI stacks while reducing operational fragmentation.
