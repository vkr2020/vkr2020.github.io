Google says Gemini has reached 1 billion monthly users, joining ChatGPT at that scale

First recorded August 11, 2026 · Latest coverage August 11, 2026 · 2 sources

Google CEO Sundar Pichai said Gemini now has 1 billion monthly users, describing it as Google’s fastest-growing product ever. The report also notes that OpenAI’s ChatGPT has already reached the same broad user scale, making Gemini another consumer AI assistant to cross the billion-user threshold.

Why it matters: A billion-user milestone signals that general-purpose AI assistants are becoming mass-market consumer products rather than niche tools. Reaching that scale strengthens the strategic position of Google and OpenAI, raises the stakes in product competition and distribution, and suggests AI interfaces are becoming core computing platforms for a very large audience.
