Lovable raises €400 million in Series C backed by the European Scaleup Fund

First recorded August 12, 2026 · Latest coverage August 12, 2026 · 2 sources

Sifted reports that AI app-building startup Lovable has raised a €400 million Series C round with backing from the European Scaleup Fund. The financing adds concrete details to earlier reporting that Lovable had completed a new round at a $13 billion valuation.

Why it matters: The round is a major capital signal for European AI application startups, pairing a large late-stage financing with support from a named EU-backed scaleup vehicle. It suggests investor conviction that Lovable is emerging as one of Europe’s most valuable AI software companies and that Europe is trying to retain and finance local AI winners at larger scale.
