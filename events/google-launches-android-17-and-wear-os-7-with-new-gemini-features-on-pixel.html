Google launches Android 17 and Wear OS 7 with new Gemini features on Pixel devices

First recorded June 16, 2026 · Latest coverage June 16, 2026 · 1 source

Google has released Android 17 and Wear OS 7, adding multitasking tools, parental controls, security features, and smartwatch upgrades. The launch is accompanied by a Pixel Drop that brings newer Gemini capabilities and AI features to supported Google devices.

Why it matters: The release extends Google's AI strategy through its core consumer operating systems and first-party hardware, not just standalone apps. By tying Gemini upgrades to Android, Wear OS, and Pixel features, Google can push AI adoption at platform scale and strengthen ecosystem lock-in.
