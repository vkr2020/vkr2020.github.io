OpenAI names Dali Rajic chief revenue officer after Denise Dresser's departure

First recorded August 13, 2026 · Latest coverage August 13, 2026 · Updated same day · 3 sources

Materially updated August 13, 2026

OpenAI has appointed Dali Rajic, previously president and chief operating officer of Wiz, as its new chief revenue officer, according to TechCrunch. The move follows the departure of Denise Dresser after less than a year in the role and adds a concrete successor to a senior commercial leadership change at the company.

Why it matters: The CRO role is central to OpenAI’s enterprise sales, partnerships, and commercialization strategy. Naming a replacement quickly turns a leadership departure into an operational transition with direct implications for go-to-market execution and for how customers and partners assess management stability at a leading AI company.
