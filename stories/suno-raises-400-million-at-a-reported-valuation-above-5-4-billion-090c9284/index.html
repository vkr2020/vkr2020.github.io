Suno raises $400 million at a reported valuation above $5.4 billion

First source report 03 Jun 2026Latest development 03 Jun 20261 source link

TechCrunch reports that AI music generation startup Suno has raised another $400 million, valuing the company at more than $5.4 billion. The round comes roughly seven months after Suno previously raised at a reported $2.45 billion valuation, even as the company continues to face copyright lawsuits.
