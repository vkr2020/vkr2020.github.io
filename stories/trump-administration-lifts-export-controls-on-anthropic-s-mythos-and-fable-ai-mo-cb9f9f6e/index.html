Trump administration lifts export controls on Anthropic's Mythos and Fable AI models

First source report 09 Jun 2026Latest development 30 Jun 20266 source links

Wired reports that the Trump administration is easing or lifting export restrictions on Anthropic's advanced Mythos and Fable AI models. The move follows a prior order that had suspended access for foreign nationals, marking a policy reversal affecting how Anthropic can make the models available internationally.
