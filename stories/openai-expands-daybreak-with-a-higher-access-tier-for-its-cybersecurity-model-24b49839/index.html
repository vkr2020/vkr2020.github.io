OpenAI expands Daybreak with a higher-access tier for its cybersecurity model

First source report 10 Aug 2026Latest development 11 Aug 20263 source links

OpenAI has added a second, higher tier to its Daybreak cybersecurity program that gives approved partners access to a more capable cyber model. Engadget reports the model is less likely to refuse higher-risk tasks, indicating a meaningful change in how OpenAI is packaging and controlling advanced defensive cyber capabilities.
