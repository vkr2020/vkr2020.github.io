OpenAI unveils its first custom inference chip, built by Broadcom

First source report 24 Jun 2026Latest development 24 Jun 20261 source link

TechCrunch reports that OpenAI has introduced its first custom chip, called Jalapeño, designed for the company’s inference workloads and built by Broadcom. The announcement suggests OpenAI is moving beyond reliance on off-the-shelf AI hardware by developing silicon tailored to its own serving infrastructure.
