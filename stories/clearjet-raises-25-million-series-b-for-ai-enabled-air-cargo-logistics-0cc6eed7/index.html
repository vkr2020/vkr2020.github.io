ClearJet raises $25 million Series B for AI-enabled air cargo logistics

First source report 12 Aug 2026Latest development 12 Aug 20261 source link

ClearJet, an Austin-based startup that uses AI to match shippers with unused cargo capacity on commercial flights, has raised a $25 million Series B round, according to Crunchbase News. The round was led by Edison Partners and is aimed at expanding the company's logistics technology platform for air freight.
