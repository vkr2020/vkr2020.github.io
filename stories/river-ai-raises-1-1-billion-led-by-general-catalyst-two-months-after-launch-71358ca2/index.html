River AI raises $1.1 billion led by General Catalyst two months after launch

First source report 11 Aug 2026Latest development 11 Aug 20261 source link

River AI, a two-month-old startup founded by xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin, has raised a $1.1 billion funding round led by General Catalyst, according to TechCrunch. The company is described as pursuing personal AI agents, making the financing an unusually large early-stage bet on an agent-focused startup and a prominent former xAI executive.
