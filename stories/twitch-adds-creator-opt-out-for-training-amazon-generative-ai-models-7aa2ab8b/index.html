Twitch adds creator opt-out for training Amazon generative AI models

First source report 12 Aug 2026Latest development 12 Aug 20265 source links

Twitch has introduced a setting that lets streamers opt out of having their streams, VODs, clips, chats, and channel text or images used in future training of Amazon generative AI models. Ars Technica reports the platform had allowed such content to be used for Amazon AI development for years under existing terms, while the new control applies prospectively rather than undoing past use.
