Coralogix raises $200 million to expand observability for AI agents

First source report 03 Jun 2026Latest development 03 Jun 20261 source link

Coralogix has raised $200 million, according to TechCrunch, as it bets that companies deploying AI agents will need stronger tools to monitor behavior, diagnose failures, and keep systems reliable in production. The funding reflects investor interest in observability infrastructure for AI applications as agentic software moves from experimentation into operational use.
