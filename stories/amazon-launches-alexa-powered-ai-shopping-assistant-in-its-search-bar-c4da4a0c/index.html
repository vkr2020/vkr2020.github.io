Amazon launches Alexa-powered AI shopping assistant in its search bar

First source report 13 May 2026Latest development 13 May 20261 source link

Amazon has launched Alexa for Shopping, an AI shopping assistant integrated into the search bar across mobile, desktop, and Echo Show devices. The assistant, powered by Alexa+, supports voice and touch interactions, offers personalized recommendations, and aims to automate shopping tasks across Amazon and other online retailers.
