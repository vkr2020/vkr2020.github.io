Spotify will label AI-only artist personas and exclude them from recommendations

First source report 11 Aug 2026Latest development 11 Aug 20262 source links

Spotify says it will begin adding an "AI Persona" badge to artist profiles that do not represent a real person and will stop recommending their music to users. The company says the change will start rolling out in mid-September, while still allowing artists to disclose that they use AI in their work.
