Groq raises $350 million at a $3.5 billion valuation as it expands its AI cloud business

First source report 29 May 2026Latest development 17 Aug 20264 source links

TechCrunch reports that Groq has raised $350 million at a $3.5 billion valuation. The financing is tied to the company's shift from being framed primarily as an AI chipmaker toward a larger AI cloud and infrastructure business, including expansion of its Nvidia-powered data center footprint.
