xAI launches Grok Bot early beta for persistent workplace AI agents

First source report 11 Aug 2026Latest development 11 Aug 20261 source link

xAI has launched an early beta of Grok Bot, a new agent product designed to operate persistently across workplace software rather than only responding to prompts. The company says users can create task-specific Bots that run in their own computer environments, keep working when a user is offline, and return with results or approval requests. VentureBeat reports the product is priced at $120 per month and grew out of internal use across functions including sales, marketing, operations, and software work.
