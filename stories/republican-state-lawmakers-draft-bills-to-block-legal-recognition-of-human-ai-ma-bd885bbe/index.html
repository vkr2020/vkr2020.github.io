Republican state lawmakers draft bills to block legal recognition of human-AI marriages

First source report 14 Aug 2026Latest development 14 Aug 20261 source link

Wired reports that some Republican state policymakers in the US are drafting legislation intended to prevent marriages between people and AI chatbots from receiving legal recognition. The article frames the effort as an early state-level policy response to emerging human-AI relationship behavior rather than a change in existing marriage law.
