Researchers disclose a now-fixed Zoom screen-sharing flaw that could let call participants take over another device

First source report 11 Aug 2026Latest development 11 Aug 20263 source links

Wired reports that researchers found and disclosed a Zoom screen-sharing vulnerability that could allow one participant on a call to seize control of another participant’s device. The flaw has since been fixed, and the article says a public AI tool helped identify the issue in fewer than 20 prompts.
