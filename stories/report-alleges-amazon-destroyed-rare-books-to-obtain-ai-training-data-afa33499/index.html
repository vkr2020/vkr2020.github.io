Report alleges Amazon destroyed rare books to obtain AI training data

First source report 17 Aug 2026Latest development 17 Aug 20262 source links

Futurism reports that Amazon used a process that involved destroying physical books, including rare volumes, in order to digitize their contents for AI training. The article frames the practice as a data-sourcing and ethics controversy around how training material was acquired, rather than a new model, product, or financing announcement.
