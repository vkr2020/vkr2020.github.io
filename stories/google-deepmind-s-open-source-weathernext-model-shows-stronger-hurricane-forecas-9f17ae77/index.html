Google DeepMind's open-source WeatherNext model shows stronger hurricane forecasting with lower-resolution data

First source report 06 Aug 2026Latest development 08 Aug 20262 source links

Ars Technica reports that Google DeepMind's open-source WeatherNext weather model delivered accurate hurricane forecasts while relying on lower-resolution input data than many conventional systems. Coverage of the work says the model gave forecasters roughly an extra day of warning in some cases, surprising weather scientists with its performance.
