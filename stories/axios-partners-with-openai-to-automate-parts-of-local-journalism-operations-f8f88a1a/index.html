Axios partners with OpenAI to automate parts of local journalism operations

First source report 16 Aug 2026Latest development 16 Aug 20261 source link

Axios and OpenAI are partnering on efforts to automate parts of local journalism operations, according to comments cited by Futurism. The reported focus is on using AI to handle non-reporting work in local markets while keeping journalists responsible for the journalism itself.
