Skan AI raises $63 million Series C and launches workflow modeling and agent products

First source report 12 Aug 2026Latest development 12 Aug 20261 source link

Skan AI announced a $63 million Series C round co-led by Cathay Innovation and Dell Technologies Capital, with participation from Citi Ventures, Bloomberg Beta, State Farm Ventures, and Wipro Ventures. The company also said it is generally releasing Skan AI Blueprint and Skan AI Agents alongside its existing Skan AI Intelligence platform, which it positions as software for observing, modeling, and automating enterprise workflows.
