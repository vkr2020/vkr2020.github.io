Research says AI could increase fossil-fuel emissions by boosting industry productivity

First source report 11 Aug 2026Latest development 11 Aug 20262 source links

Wired reports on new research arguing that AI adoption in fossil-fuel operations could increase carbon emissions by making extraction and production more efficient. The study says the resulting emissions increase could substantially exceed the direct climate impact associated with AI data-center power use.
