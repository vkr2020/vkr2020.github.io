Nvidia reportedly commits $6 billion with Poolside to build a U.S.-based open AI ecosystem

First source report 23 Aug 2026Latest development 23 Aug 20261 source link

The Wall Street Journal reports that Nvidia has reached a sweeping agreement with startup Poolside involving $6 billion to help build a U.S.-based open AI ecosystem. The deal is framed as an effort to create a domestic alternative that could compete with leading Chinese AI players as well as major U.S. AI companies.
