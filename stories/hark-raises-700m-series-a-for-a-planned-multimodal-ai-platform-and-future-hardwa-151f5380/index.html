Hark raises $700M Series A for a planned multimodal AI platform and future hardware

First source report 21 May 2026Latest development 21 May 20261 source link

Hark has raised a $700 million Series A, according to TechCrunch, as the secretive startup works on what it describes as a universal AI interface. The company says it plans to release its first multimodal models this summer to power a personal AI platform that works with existing products and services, followed later by dedicated hardware devices.
