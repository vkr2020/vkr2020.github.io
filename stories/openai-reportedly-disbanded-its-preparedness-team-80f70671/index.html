OpenAI reportedly disbanded its preparedness team

First source report 16 Aug 2026Latest development 16 Aug 20262 source links

The Financial Times, as cited by The Verge, reports that OpenAI disbanded its preparedness team at the end of last month. The team was responsible for assessing whether models posed serious risks and for developing mitigations, with those responsibilities reportedly being reassigned elsewhere inside the company.
