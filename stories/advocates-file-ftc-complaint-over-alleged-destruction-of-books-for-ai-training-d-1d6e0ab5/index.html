Advocates file FTC complaint over alleged destruction of books for AI training data

First source report 21 Aug 2026Latest development 21 Aug 20261 source link

A group of advocates has filed a complaint with the US Federal Trade Commission alleging that AI companies obtained training data by destroying physical books to digitize them. The complaint turns a reported data-sourcing controversy into a formal regulatory matter focused on consumer protection, copyright-adjacent concerns, and preservation issues.
