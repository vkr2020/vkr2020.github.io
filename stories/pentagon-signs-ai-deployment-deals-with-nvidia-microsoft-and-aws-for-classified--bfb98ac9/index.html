Pentagon signs AI deployment deals with Nvidia, Microsoft, and AWS for classified networks

First source report 01 May 2026Latest development 01 May 20261 source link

The U.S. Department of Defense has signed agreements with Nvidia, Microsoft, and Amazon Web Services to deploy AI capabilities on classified networks, according to TechCrunch. The report says the move reflects the Pentagon's effort to broaden its roster of AI vendors following a dispute with Anthropic over model usage terms.
