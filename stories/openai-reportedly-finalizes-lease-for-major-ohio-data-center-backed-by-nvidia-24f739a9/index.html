OpenAI reportedly finalizes lease for major Ohio data center backed by Nvidia

First source report 17 Aug 2026Latest development 17 Aug 20261 source link

The Wall Street Journal reports that OpenAI has locked in a lease for a large data-center project in Ohio in a deal involving SoftBank-owned SB Energy and backing from Nvidia. The agreement is described as the largest data-center project announced to date and caps weeks of negotiations over a major AI infrastructure buildout.
