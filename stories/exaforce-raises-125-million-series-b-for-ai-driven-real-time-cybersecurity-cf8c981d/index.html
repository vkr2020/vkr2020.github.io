Exaforce raises $125 million Series B for AI-driven real-time cybersecurity

First source report 12 May 2026Latest development 12 May 20261 source link

Exaforce has raised a $125 million Series B round to build AI systems designed to detect and stop cyberattacks as they happen, according to TechCrunch. The report says the three-year-old startup was valued at $725 million in the financing, reflecting investor interest in security tools built for faster AI-enabled threat activity.
