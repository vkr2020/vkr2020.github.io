Nvidia and major Wall Street firms form AI financing partnership targeting up to $500 billion

First source report 10 Aug 2026Latest development 10 Aug 20261 source link

Nvidia and a group of large financial firms including Apollo, Blackstone, and BlackRock have struck an AI financing deal aimed at supporting as much as $500 billion in investment. Based on the report, the partnership is structured to mobilize large-scale capital for AI-related infrastructure and deployment rather than fund a single startup financing round.
