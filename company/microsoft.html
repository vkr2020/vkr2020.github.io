  1. August 20, 2026 · Products · 1 source

    Microsoft adds per-process NPU monitoring to Windows Task Manager

    Microsoft is adding per-process neural processing unit monitoring to Windows Task Manager, giving users a way to view AI-related hardware usage at the process level. The feature extends the long-running Windows utility to surface NPU workload activity alongside other system resource metrics.

  2. August 17, 2026 · Products · 1 source

    Microsoft says Exchange Server update is delayed amid AI-related bug backlog

    Microsoft has delayed a promised update for Exchange Server and, according to The Register, attributed the slippage in part to a backlog of bugs generated by AI-assisted development or testing workflows. The company reportedly did not provide a new delivery date, turning the issue into a product-status update rather than a launch announcement.

  3. August 13, 2026 · Products · 2 sources

    Microsoft removes Mico from Copilot voice mode and shifts the avatar to Learn Live

    Microsoft is removing the Mico animated character from Copilot's voice mode and plans to move the avatar to its Learn Live platform, according to a Microsoft support page cited by The Verge. Mico had served as an expressive visual companion for voice interactions in Copilot after launching there in 2025.

  4. August 13, 2026 · Products · 1 source

    Microsoft begins unifying its Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot apps

    Microsoft is starting to combine its consumer and commercial Copilot assistants into a single app experience. According to The Verge, users of the Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot apps will be moved into a unified Microsoft Copilot app that supports both personal and work accounts and uses updated branding.

  5. July 2, 2026 · Companies · 1 source

    Microsoft launches an AI deployment company with a $2.5 billion commitment

    Microsoft has launched a new AI deployment company backed by a reported $2.5 billion commitment, according to TechCrunch. The effort appears aimed at helping customers implement AI systems in practice, following similar deployment-focused moves described for Amazon, OpenAI, and Anthropic.

  6. June 1, 2026 · Products · 1 source

    Nvidia pushes AI agent PCs with Microsoft, Dell, and HP

    TechCrunch reports that Nvidia is promoting a new push into AI-focused personal computers built around AI agents, working with Microsoft, Dell, and HP. The effort is framed as an attempt to extend Nvidia's AI position beyond datacenter accelerators and into the much larger PC and CPU market.

  7. May 1, 2026 · Policy · 1 source

    Pentagon signs AI deployment deals with Nvidia, Microsoft, and AWS for classified networks

    The U.S. Department of Defense has signed agreements with Nvidia, Microsoft, and Amazon Web Services to deploy AI capabilities on classified networks, according to TechCrunch. The report says the move reflects the Pentagon's effort to broaden its roster of AI vendors following a dispute with Anthropic over model usage terms.
