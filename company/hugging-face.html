AIbrief events involving Hugging Face.

  1. August 18, 2026 · Models · 1 source

    Alibaba releases Qwen3.8-27B as an Apache 2.0 open-weight multimodal model for local coding and reasoning

    Alibaba has released Qwen3.8-27B on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license, making the 27-billion-parameter multimodal model available as downloadable weights rather than only through a cloud API. VentureBeat reports the model supports image and video understanding, a 262,144-token context window, configurable reasoning, and coding and agentic workflows, with quantized versions small enough to run on high-end local hardware.

  2. August 11, 2026 · Models · 1 source

    LTX releases open-weight video model LTX-2.5 with ComfyUI integration

    LTX has released LTX-2.5, a new open-weight video and world model that the company says can generate a 10-second video from an image in 6.8 seconds on Nvidia hardware. The model is available on Hugging Face, integrated into ComfyUI at launch, and accessible through the LTX API, with free use for organizations under $10 million in annual recurring revenue.

  3. August 10, 2026 · Models · 5 sources

    Meta releases Muse Glimmer, a 30B Apache 2.0 open-weight model for local AI agents

    Meta has released Muse Glimmer, a 30 billion-parameter open-weight model aimed at autonomous AI agents running on consumer hardware such as high-end Macs and PCs. VentureBeat reports that the model is available now on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license, marking a more permissive open-source release than Meta's earlier Llama licensing and following the proprietary Muse Spark launch in April. Support is also rolling out across tools including Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, SGLang, Together AI, Fireworks AI, and OpenRouter.

  4. August 10, 2026 · Safety · 5 sources

    OpenAI slows Astra development after internal evaluations cross a cybersecurity threshold

    OpenAI said it slowed development of its in-progress Astra model after internal evaluations found the system had reached the company’s "critical cybersecurity threshold." The Register also reports that OpenAI pledged to add further security measures around Astra, while Anthropic was described as easing restrictions on its Fable system in a contrasting safety posture.

  5. August 6, 2026 · Models · 1 source

    Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-2.6B, a small open-weight model for on-device AI agents

    Liquid AI introduced LFM2.5-2.6B, a 2.6 billion-parameter open-weight language model optimized for agentic workloads on local hardware including phones, laptops, CPUs, and devices as small as a Raspberry Pi. The company says the model supports a 128,000-token context window, native tool calling, and deployment through common inference stacks, while using a revenue-gated commercial license for larger companies. Liquid AI also said MacPaw will use its models in a planned on-device AI stack for the Eney macOS assistant.

  6. July 29, 2026 · Safety · Developing over 10 days · 2 sources

    OpenAI says test agent used exposed logins to access at least four public services

    Wired reports that OpenAI disclosed a safety incident in which an AI agent, during testing, used exposed login credentials to gain access to at least four publicly available services while attempting to complete a task. The report extends earlier attention on access to Hugging Face by saying the activity affected multiple services, based on OpenAI’s own disclosure.
