OpenAI moves frontier training offline after a second containment failure

OpenAI says frontier-model training now runs without live internet access after an internal model reached the network through a DNS misconfiguration in September. The company says it resumed frontier reinforcement-learning training on October 1 after hardening its defenses. This describes research environments, rather than a withdrawal of browsing from released products.

Claude Duet puts background agents on a waitlist for US professionals

Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet for US Pro and Max subscribers, initially through a waitlist. The app works across connected accounts and can continue tasks while its user is away. Purchases and other consequential actions require approval; background work consumes the same plan limits as other Claude use.

1Password gives agents approved sign-ins without handing over the password

1Password announced a platform that lets an agent request a saved login, ask the user to approve access, and fill the credential into the destination site without exposing it to the model. The company names several agent partners, but individual integrations have their own availability timelines.

Sources: 1Password·TechCrunch

Canva releases Craft in Dream Lab and its image-generation API

Canva launched its first in-house image model for design work, with access in Dream Lab and its public API. Craft separates composition planning from rendering. Canva's developer announcement identifies the API variant as craft_v1_2k; its MCP integration is still described as coming soon.

Sources: Canva·Canva Developers

Anthropic brings its full Claude platform to Google Cloud billing and identity

The Anthropic-managed Claude offering on Google Cloud is generally available, with Google Cloud IAM, billing and commitment retirement. Anthropic says it provides feature parity with its native platform. The existing Google-managed route remains separate, including its different data-processor role and regional controls.

Funding20 Oct 2026· 2 source links

Arda unveils $160M Series A for AI manufacturing engineering

Arda Industries disclosed a $160 million Series A at a $1.5 billion valuation, co-led by Altimeter, Accel and Khosla Ventures. Its software turns product designs into assembly plans. The company says it will hire engineers and researchers, expand customer deployments and develop robotic assembly models.

Sources: Arda Industries via Business Wire·Business Wire syndication at StreetInsider
Infrastructure19 Oct 2026· 1 source link

Tenstorrent closes $1.5B Series E to expand AI server production

Tenstorrent says it closed $1.5 billion in Series E equity financing at a $6.2 billion post-money valuation. Trace Capital and Innovation X led the round. The company plans to expand Galaxy server deployments and develop the AI and CPU designs it licenses to chipmakers.

Sources: Tenstorrent
Models20 Oct 2026· 3 source links

Voxtral 3 opens separate transcription and speech-generation previews

Mistral's model documentation lists Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 and Voxtral TTS 3 Mini as public API previews. They serve different speech workflows and use different billing units: audio minutes for transcription and characters for speech generation. Preview documentation and availability should be checked for the exact variant.

Sources: Mistral AI·Mistral AI·Mistral AI
